# The two axes as fitted on 8 October 2026

The 28 houses' homepage pictures then fetched (2,039 placed, mostly October 2022 to October 2026), read on
tone-v2 and compared on the two axes kept by the bake-off (intimate, provocative) as probabilities, by
Qwen3-VL-32B at the weights in `data/luxury/readers.json`.

- `pairs.json`: that fit's comparison design (every picture against six others on each axis)
- `positions-qwen3-v2.jsonl`: each picture's place on each axis
- `luxury-v2.json`: the readings as they stood (`data/results/luxury-v2.json` at commit 796933d)
- `corpus.json`: the corpus as rebuilt on 9 October (3,009 pictures; 2,039 of them were in the fit)

Kept so the refit on the full history (2014 to 2026, all 40 houses, from 16 October) can be set beside it.
The readings files in `data/luxury/readings/` are shared: a picture's tone-v2 answers and a pair's
probabilities do not depend on the fit, and the refit reuses them.
