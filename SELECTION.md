# Panel selection, v1

Chosen 5 October 2026: ten treated houses and eight controls.

## Treated

Each had a creative director's first show between July 2025 and February 2026: Chanel (Matthieu
Blazy), Dior (Jonathan Anderson), Gucci (Demna), Balenciaga (Pierpaolo Piccioli), Loewe (Jack
McCollough and Lazaro Hernandez), Bottega Veneta (Louise Trotter), Celine (Michael Rider), Fendi
(Maria Grazia Chiuri), Jil Sander (Simone Bellotti) and Maison Margiela (Glenn Martens).

Ads collected in October 2026 reach back to about October 2025, because the repository keeps an ad
for a year after it last ran. Debuts in this range leave some advertising on both sides of the
change. The July debuts (Celine, Margiela) are kept even though they are expected to have too little
pre-change advertising: they will report as insufficient rather than being dropped after the fact.

Gucci has two candidate dates: the first presentation (a lookbook and film, September 2025) and the
first runway show (early 2026). The registry uses the presentation, because the new direction was
public from then.

Balenciaga and Gucci form the H2 pair. Demna left Balenciaga for Gucci, and Piccioli succeeded him.

## Controls

These houses had stable creative leadership through the window: Hermès, Louis Vuitton, Prada, Miu
Miu, Saint Laurent, Burberry, Valentino and Loro Piana.

They come from the same owners as the treated houses where possible (LVMH, Kering, Prada Group), so
a group-level change in media buying or agency does not read as a designer effect. Valentino's
director arrived in 2024, before the window.

Controls were checked for creative leadership changes inside the window:

- **Saint Laurent.** Reported on 30 September 2026 that Anthony Vaccarello leaves after the Spring
  2027 show; Saint Laurent had not confirmed it. His tenure covers the whole retained window, so the
  house stays a control for the retrospective analysis. Once a successor is named it becomes the
  natural first forward case.
- **Hermès.** Womenswear is unchanged. Menswear changes hands: Véronique Nichanian's last collection
  was January 2026, and Grace Wales Bonner's first is due January 2027, after the window. Kept as a
  control; the menswear transition is noted.
- **Louis Vuitton, Burberry, Valentino.** No change found. (Valentino changed chief executive in
  2025, not creative director.)
- **Prada, Miu Miu, Loro Piana.** No change found in searches through October 2026.

Still to do before the registry is frozen: confirm each house's page ids. Page ids must be fashion
pages only: a beauty or fragrance page, whether run by the house or a licensee, carries a different
tone and a different budget.

## Watch houses

Chloé is collected but sits outside the v1 panel: never in the field, never in the tests. Speculation
names its creative director as Saint Laurent's next, and if that happens the forward test needs
Chloé's advertising from before the move, which can only be archived in advance.
