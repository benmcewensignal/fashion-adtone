Focal measures the character of fashion brands, how it shifts, and what it contributes to their success.

What it shows

Character

What is the brand's image?

Eighteen measured qualities of its advertising, from light and setting to mood, and the cast of people who make it.

Shift

How is it changing?

How far the image moves from its own past and against its peers, how consistent it stays, and whether it is drifting towards the brand of the moment.

Alignment

Does the advertising say what the brand said at the show?

Question by question, beside the same check for its peers.

Contribution

What does it add to success?

Attention, desire and revenue beyond what the brand's own momentum and the reach it paid for would deliver, down to whether a show's attention lasts and the campaign carries it.

Each image is read against eighteen fixed questions, from light and setting to mood, and kept as its answers and a numerical fingerprint. Every brand is measured against its peers month by month, so a move by the whole market doesn't read as a move by one brand.

It sees paid advertising on Meta's platforms in Europe and Britain, not print. Contribution builds with the record: each month adds attention, each quarter adds desire and revenue.

Who makes the image

A brand's image is made by a cast: the designer, and the photographers, stylists and directors around them. Focal records who made each campaign, so a brand's character is read with the people behind it.

What we offer

Alignment check

Does the advertising say what the brand says?

Show notes and campaign statements are read into the same eighteen questions as the ads. The check reports, question by question, where the advertising carries the stated intent and where it parts from it, set beside the same check for the brand's peers.

Per brand, per season

Insights

How is each brand doing?

A monthly reading of every brand: its character and how it shifted, where it sits among its peers, who is making its image, and how its attention, desire and revenue moved alongside. Each reading comes with its uncertainty.

Monthly

For brands

How the brand's character reads today, which way it is moving, and what it adds to attention and demand, beside its peers.

For investors

Whether a brand's image is strengthening or drifting, on a dated record, before results are reported.

Neither is a sales forecast. Both start once the advertising is collected.
