# Ambassadors

`reference/ambassadors.csv` lists ambassador appointments announced by the 28 houses from 2019 to
October 2026: the face in front of the camera, which the reader never identifies in a picture, kept
as dated events beside the creative leads in `reference/designers.csv`. Compiled 7 October 2026 from
public announcements and the fashion press; every row cites at least one page that states the
appointment, and `verified` is true only where that page also gives the date.

What to keep in mind:

- Dates are mostly when a report appeared, not the brand's own release, and appointments announced in
  Asia can sit a day off in European time. 187 rows are dated to the day, 10 to the month.
- End dates are rarely reported: a blank end is unknown, not ongoing.
- Coverage leans to recent years (36 to 40 a year from 2023, 12 in 2019 and 2020) and to talent
  announced for Asian markets. The largest houses have more ambassadors than are listed.
- Hermès, Brunello Cucinelli, Jil Sander, Dries Van Noten and Alaïa have no formal programme found; the
  note rows say so from searches, not from a brand statement. Tom Ford's rows are all Tom Ford Beauty.
- Beauty, fragrance and corporate roles are kept in the file but left out of the events
  (`adtone.readings.ambassador_events`): the first fashion ambassador a brand names in each calendar
  year, verified, and not within a year of a change of creative lead at the same brand.
